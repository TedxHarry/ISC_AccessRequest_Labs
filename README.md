# SailPoint ISC Access Requests: Hands-On Labs

Learn to configure, request, approve, deliver and remove access by doing the work in an ISC training tenant and Active Directory.

**[Start here](START-HERE.md)** · [Open Guided Labs](guided-labs/README.md)

## Follow the guided course

Build the environment once in AR-001 to AR-009. Then follow the everyday-work sequence below. If the Acme course environment is already prepared, begin with [AR-009's readiness checks](guided-labs/M01/AR-009/README.md) before moving to the first request.

| Module | What you will do | Labs |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | [Prepare the environment](guided-labs/M01/README.md) | AR-001 to AR-009 |
| 02 | [Complete your first requests](guided-labs/M02/README.md) | AR-010 to AR-015 |
| 03 | [Build business access](guided-labs/M03/README.md) | AR-016 to AR-021 |
| 04 | [Control visibility and account selection](guided-labs/M04/README.md) | AR-022 to AR-027 |
| 05 | [Configure approvals](guided-labs/M05/README.md) | AR-028 to AR-033 |
| 06 | [Collect request information](guided-labs/M06/README.md) | AR-034 to AR-039 |
| 07 | [Manage temporary access and removal](guided-labs/M07/README.md) | AR-040 to AR-045 |
| 08 | [Investigate fulfillment](guided-labs/M08/README.md) | AR-046 to AR-051 |
| 12 | [Handle daily operations](guided-labs/M12/README.md) | AR-070 to AR-075 |

Forms and access-date exercises depend on the features available in your tenant. The [lab map](guided-labs/README.md) gives the specific alternatives; you can still complete the core grant, denial, removal and operations work.

## Practice after the guided labs

| Area | Use it for |
|---|---|
| [Troubleshooting Practice](troubleshooting/README.md) | Diagnose task-specific problems and compare your reasoning with worked cases. You can also consult it if you get stuck. |
| [Try It Yourself](try-it-yourself/README.md) | Repeat a skill with a different person or requirement and less guidance. |
| [Capstones](guided-labs/README.md#capstones) | Deliver a complete service or resolve an operations scenario. Each capstone lists its prerequisites. |

Finish the regular labs before taking on the extra practice. No journal or screenshot submission is required.

## What you need

Use an ISC training tenant, an isolated AD lab, working connector infrastructure, and separate administrator, requester and reviewer sessions. This course prepares the Acme identities and access configuration. Installing the underlying tenant, virtual appliance and IQService is a prerequisite, not a walkthrough included here.
