## Common problems

| What you see | First check | What proves recovery |
|---|---|---|
| A feature control is absent | Check that specific lab's capability, supported source and permission requirements | The required feature is available, or that extension is left unexecuted |
| Eligibility is mistaken for active access | Compare eligibility, activation history, effective dates and native membership | The observed activation or removal is established on the intended account |
| An asynchronous callback did not complete the request | Match the invocation, deadline and callback status without exposing its secret URL | The actual invocation result and resulting request/native outcome are verified separately |
