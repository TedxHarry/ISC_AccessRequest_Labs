## Common problems

| What you see | First check | What proves recovery |
|---|---|---|
| The administrator sees an item but the employee does not | Inspect the employee's current identity value, segment criteria and item requestability | The intended employee sees the item without widening visibility for unrelated users |
| A manager cannot select an employee | Check the signed-in manager, resolved manager relationship and request-on-behalf setting | The correct eligible recipient can be selected without broadening global authority unnecessarily |
| The request targets the wrong account | Match the interactive account choice to the recorded account name and DN | The request, native operation and intended account all agree |

## AR-024 · Separate requester visibility from recipient eligibility

[Open the guided lab](../guided-labs/AR-024/README.md). Use that lab's people and objects. Numbered Section references below refer to its walkthrough. Preserve later legitimate accounts and assignments when revisiting an earlier lab.

### If the result differs

If you stop before completing the denial, restore the original request-on-behalf setting using Section 5 and record the outstanding request ID and stage. Restoring the setting does not cancel an already-submitted request; finish its review or use the [existing-request procedure](../M02-READINESS.md#account-for-existing-requests-before-repeating) before another attempt.

If the profile is unavailable to Lucas, verify his session, Finance identity attribute and the segment before loosening visibility. If it was accidentally approved, do not report the denial exercise as passed: verify Liam's actual groups, remove the requested profile through the AR-016 removal procedure using Liam as recipient and Daniel as reviewer, then repeat from a clean state with a new request ID. Preserve Lucas's grant.

### Diagnose this ticket

This supplied practice case is separate from your observed tenant results. Write the first inspection, likely cause, smallest correction and repeat check before opening the answer.

<details>
<summary>Compare your diagnosis</summary>

The check reads the wrong identity for recipient eligibility. Separate requester authority/visibility from the requested-for business rule. Use the request's recipient identifier to inspect Liam's department and deny the supplied case. This lab demonstrates the manual decision only; do not claim automated enforcement until a later workflow or policy has been implemented and tested.

</details>

## AR-027 · Verify visibility and account targeting

[Open the guided lab](../guided-labs/AR-027/README.md). Use that lab's people and objects. Numbered Section references below refer to its walkthrough. Preserve later legitimate accounts and assignments when revisiting an earlier lab.

### If the result differs

If a catalog result differs, find the first mismatch in identity data, segment membership or item settings before changing anything. If an account result differs, compare the written intent, submitted account, activity target and both native accounts. Complete removal of the exact requested assignment before another test; do not clear memberships directly in AD.

### Diagnose this ticket

This supplied practice case is separate from your observed tenant results. Write the first inspection, likely cause, smallest correction and repeat check before opening the answer.

> Three reports arrive together: Liam cannot find Finance Reporting; Lucas cannot see it while an org administrator can, and his identity says Sales; Sofia intended the second account but the recorded submission selected standard and the operation updated standard. Someone proposes rerunning every aggregation.

<details>
<summary>Compare your diagnosis</summary>

Separate the cases. Liam's Finance restriction matches policy when his IT identity and control catalog are correct. Lucas needs the source-to-identity department chain checked and corrected, followed by a requester retest; administrator visibility is exempt from segmentation. Sofia's operation matches the submitted selection, so inspect the selection/review error, remove the exact wrong test assignment and repeat with the intended account. Rerunning every aggregation does not correct all three causes. Use each case's own before/after and restoration evidence.

</details>
