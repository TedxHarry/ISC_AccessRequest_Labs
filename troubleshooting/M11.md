## Common problems

| What you see | First check | What proves recovery |
|---|---|---|
| No subscriber invocation is observed | Check the dedicated subscription, filter and controlled reachable service | A fresh matching request produces a correlated invocation |
| The service logged a request but ISC did not accept its decision | Inspect response mode, required payload, response timing and invocation status | ISC records the accepted response and the request outcome is checked separately |
| A notification was mistaken for a pending approval | Identify the actual event type and where it occurs relative to the decision | Use the event for its documented role and inspect the real request's current decision |
