# CAP-01 · Launch Finance self-service access

**Prerequisites:** AR-001–045. Add AR-061 for automated recipient eligibility and AR-076–077 when SoD is available.

Finance needs Reporting and Accounts Payable choices, a combined Analyst role, appropriate review and verified removal.

## Deliver the service

1. Record your starting checkpoint, Finance identities, existing assignments and native group memberships. Choose Olivia as the clean requester/recipient.
2. Inspect or build the two Finance profiles, Finance Services application and Finance Analyst role using AR-016–020. Compare each exact entitlement list with the business requirement before enabling requests.
3. Set the required owner/reviewer policy and comments. Record the effective configuration, including any inherited settings or workflow association.
4. Use the Finance segment to test Olivia's visibility against Liam's. Test authorized requests for others and record whether recipient eligibility is enforced by the configured decision process.
5. Submit and approve Reporting for Olivia. Prove both required native memberships, then remove the assignment and verify the reset.
6. Submit and deny Accounts Payable. Prove no new grant.
7. Request the Analyst role and verify its three underlying groups. Remove the role and explain any remaining assignment path.
8. Inject the application-association fault from AR-020. Diagnose it using Olivia's session and repair it without recreating the profiles.
9. If the SoD track is available, include its conflicting and clean request results. Do not assume the illustrative payment-duty policy applies to other Finance groups without configuring it.
10. Save a handover with object IDs, owners, request/removal policy, positive/negative evidence and reconstruction instructions.

## Acceptance

Pass when the business choices are understandable, authorized users can request them, required decisions occur, target access matches the selected package and cleanup preserves unrelated access. A visibility-only segment does not satisfy a recipient-eligibility requirement.

## Screenshots to capture

1. Application, profiles, role and entitlement lists.
2. Finance/non-Finance requester comparison.
3. Approved, denied and removed requests with native evidence.
4. Fault, repair and final handover.
