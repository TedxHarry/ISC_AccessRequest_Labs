## Hands-on challenge: Handle denial before delivering a fresh request

**Start with:** Use Liam, Priya and AP-Remote-Worker after completing the regular course. Liam has baseline access but no VPN/Remote Users membership or pending Remote Worker request. Inspect the retained profile: Priya owns it and reviews grants and removals; no form or date requirement is configured.

**Your task:** Submit a new Liam request with reason CH-M02-deny and deny it as Priya. Then submit a separate CH-M02-approve request, approve it, verify both native groups, and request removal. Have Priya approve the removal. Use the current request/account lookup procedures without copying the earlier submission walkthrough.

**You have succeeded when:** Three separate requests explain denial, grant and removal. Denial grants nothing; approval adds both business groups to Liam's original account; removal clears both. Baseline and Lucas's retained VPN stay unchanged.

<details>
<summary>Show a hint</summary>

A denial is not a failed grant to retry. The later approved request needs its own ID and activity. Inspect each group after approval and removal.

</details>

**Finish:** Resolve all three requests. Leave Liam without the two business groups and preserve the original profile settings.
