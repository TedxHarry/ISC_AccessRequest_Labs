## Hands-on challenge: Repeat manual fulfillment from the application register

**Start with:** Complete AR-078 first. The separate Acme Disconnected Reports source has D012 for Lucas and D025 for Taylor. Keep the complete current two-row application register separate from Acme HR and retain the saved requestability/reviewer configuration.

**Your task:** Using AR-078's supported request/removal path, temporarily make the retained Reader item available as required, request it for Lucas, have Samuel fulfill the application's register, complete the task and reconcile ISC. Then remove Lucas's Reader through the same supported process and reconcile again. Preserve Taylor's Reader row throughout.

**You have succeeded when:** A review/task state, the simulated application's register and the imported account are checked separately. Lucas's grant and removal are reflected in the two-row register and ISC; no new identity or AD account is created.
