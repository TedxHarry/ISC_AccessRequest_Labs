Complete the guided labs first. Then attempt these variations without copying the walkthrough. Use the expandable answers after making your own prediction or diagnosis. You can explain your reasoning aloud; no journal or submission is required.

## Hands-on challenge: Test the form from its saved rules

**Start with:** Native forms are available and the Production Support form remains attached. Use Henry and keep his native access unchanged.

**Your task:** Inspect the saved required questions and conditional rule. In a fresh request draft, first try an omitted required answer. Then supply valid inputs and compare the field behavior for Production and the configured nonmatching environment. Predict each result before changing the answer. Leave the request flow without submitting when finished.

**You have succeeded when:** The required-answer check and both condition branches match the saved form. You distinguish validation in a request draft from a submitted, reviewed or provisioned request. No new request or native grant remains.

<details>
<summary>Show a hint</summary>

Read the condition operator, comparison value and effect together. A field appearing or disappearing is not proof of a target provisioning rule.

</details>

**Finish:** Discard the draft. Preserve the original form definition, association and profile review/date settings.
