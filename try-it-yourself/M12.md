Complete the guided labs first. Then attempt these variations without copying the walkthrough. Use the expandable answers after making your own prediction or diagnosis. You can explain your reasoning aloud; no journal or submission is required.

## Hands-on challenge: Resolve a fresh support report

**Start with:** The core operations setup and AR-075 cleanup are complete. Use the separate live operations cases with Taylor, the current Remote Worker profile and the original settings before each exercise.

**Your task:** Choose a live case, perform its isolated setup, then diagnose from the requester's report. Identify the smallest supported repair and verify both the user-facing result and native state. Explain why your evidence establishes recovery rather than merely repeating the expected answer.

**You have succeeded when:** The symptom is actually reproduced, the correction matches an observed difference, and a fresh justified control works. Pending tasks and test access are resolved; historical cases are not relabelled as current outages.
