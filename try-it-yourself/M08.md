## Hands-on challenge: Reconcile a controlled native change

**Start with:** Use Taylor and the disposable group established in AR-049, identified by its retained current DN/GUID. The group grants no real privilege, Taylor has no existing membership or assignment supplying it, and course subscriptions are disabled. Follow AR-050's native-change and reconciliation methods.

**Your task:** Add Taylor directly to that disposable AD group, inspect native and currently imported values, then aggregate and verify reconciliation. Remove the same direct membership and reconcile again. Predict which view can lag before each refresh; do not manually grant a business group or change a role.

**You have succeeded when:** The actual before/after observations establish which view changed and when. Imported membership eventually matches each completed native change for the same account/group. A lag not actually observed is not invented.
