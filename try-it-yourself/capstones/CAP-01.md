Try this after the named guided prerequisites. Use the requirement and acceptance checks first; open hints only when needed. No journal or screenshot submission is required.

## Starting conditions

Complete the Finance setup and supported role/profile removal labs. Olivia is clean of Finance test access; Liam is the non-Finance visibility control. Preserve accepted Finance policies and Lucas's existing VPN.

## Your assignment

Finance needs a catalog offering Reporting and Accounts Payable separately, plus a Finance Analyst role containing both. Use the existing Finance objects. Prove Reporting grant/removal, an Accounts Payable denial and Analyst role grant/removal for Olivia. Compare Olivia's visible choices with Liam's. Inspect the actual review policy before predicting reviewers.

## Acceptance checks

- The application offers the two intended profiles and the role has the intended contents.
- Each test request has its own observed decision, operation and native result.
- Finance requester visibility is tested separately from recipient eligibility. Do not claim automated recipient eligibility or SoD unless you actually perform their additional tests.
- Olivia finishes without capstone business access, Lucas retains VPN, original removal settings are restored and accepted catalog/grant policies remain.
